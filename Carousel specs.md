### Carousel specs

Responsive layout: 
- in desktop occupy one (middle) column of a three column layout (max width c 480px)
- in mobile full width

Positioning: Show a single item that snaps to the center of the carousel window, with the edges of the last / next items also visible at the sides of the carousel. Handle cases with only one or two observations in the list 

Navigation: include back / next buttons and a simple "n of total" pagination indicator

Touch events: support swipe back and forth

Performance: support lazy loading for images (v-lazy-load plugin?)
